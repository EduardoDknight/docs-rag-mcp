# Security Incident Response

If you suspect a security incident, report it immediately to security@example.com
or the #security-incidents chat channel. Do not try to investigate on your own.

## Severity levels
SEV1: customer data exposed or production down. Response within 15 minutes.
SEV2: degraded service or suspected compromise. Response within 1 hour.
SEV3: minor issue with no customer impact. Response within 1 business day.
