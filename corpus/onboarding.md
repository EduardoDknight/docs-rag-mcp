# New Employee Onboarding

## Week one
On day one you receive your laptop, badge and accounts for email, chat and the HR portal.
Your onboarding buddy schedules a welcome meeting and a product walkthrough.

## Security training
Every new hire must complete security awareness training within the first 14 days.
Training covers phishing, password managers and reporting incidents to security@example.com.

## 90-day review
Your manager holds a 90-day review to set goals for the rest of the year.
