# Customer Refund Policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase.
After 30 days and up to 90 days, refunds are issued as store credit only.
No refunds are issued after 90 days.

## How refunds are processed
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Annual subscriptions cancelled mid-term are refunded pro rata for unused full months.
Support agents may approve refunds up to 200 USD; larger amounts need a team lead.
