# HR Policy: Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month, up to 18 days per year.
Unused PTO carries over to the next year up to a maximum of 5 days.

## Requesting time off
Submit PTO requests in the HR portal at least 10 business days in advance.
Your direct manager approves or rejects the request within 3 business days.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO: employees get 6 paid sick days per year.
A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
